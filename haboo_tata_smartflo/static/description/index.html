Overview

Every SmartFlo call, logged against the right lead

Cloud telephony only pays off when call data lands next to the deal it belongs to. This module connects Tata SmartFlo to Odoo CRM, enabling click-to-call dialing straight from a lead record and automatically pulling call detail records (CDR) back into that same lead's history through a scheduled cron sync.

A CDR history view gives managers a searchable log of every call - duration, direction, and outcome - without needing to check the SmartFlo dashboard separately.

Process

From dial to logged call record

1
Configure SmartFlo credentials

Enter your Tata SmartFlo API credentials once under Settings, connecting Odoo to your cloud telephony account.

2
Click to dial from a lead

Call directly from any CRM lead record using click-to-call, with the call routed through SmartFlo.

3
CDR syncs back automatically

A scheduled cron job pulls call detail records back into Odoo, logging duration, direction, and outcome against the originating lead.

Features

Everything the module gives you

Click-to-Call From CRM

Dial any lead's phone number directly from its Odoo record - no separate SmartFlo dashboard required.

Call History View

A dedicated CDR history view shows every call's duration, direction, and outcome in one searchable list.

A SmartFlo-specific access group keeps telephony data visible only to the right users.

Opportunity Conversion Wizard

Built for Odoo CRM
