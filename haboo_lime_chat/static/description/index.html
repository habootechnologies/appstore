Overview

Chatbot conversations that turn into pipeline

LimeChat automates the first response, but the moment a conversation is worth a human follow-up, it needs to land somewhere your sales team actually works. This module connects Odoo to LimeChat and stores every chatbot and support conversation as a structured record, with an opportunity conversion wizard purpose-built for the handoff.

A dedicated security group keeps LimeChat data visible only to the right users, and every conversation can be converted straight into a CRM opportunity without retyping contact details.
