Overview

Run Shopify and Odoo as one system, not two

Selling on Shopify while running operations in Odoo usually means double data entry - products in two places, orders re-keyed, inventory drifting out of sync. This module connects the two directly, syncing products, customers, orders, and fulfillment through scheduled cron jobs, so Shopify stays your storefront and Odoo stays your system of record.

Abandoned checkouts sync into CRM as opportunities, giving your sales team a chance to recover lost carts through a proper follow-up wizard instead of relying on Shopify's generic recovery emails alone.
