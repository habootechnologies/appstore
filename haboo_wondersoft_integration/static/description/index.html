Overview

Wondersoft and Odoo, kept in step automatically

Retail and POS operations running on Wondersoft often need Odoo for CRM, sales, or reporting - but keeping product, customer, and inventory data aligned between the two takes real engineering. This module handles that sync directly, moving products, customers, inventory, and transaction data between Odoo and Wondersoft through a dedicated credential model and scheduled cron jobs.

Dedicated views for Wondersoft products, customers, and orders let your team review synced data without opening the Wondersoft system directly, and CRM leads connect straight through to the transaction history behind them.
