Overview

Every Facebook and Instagram interaction, tracked in one CRM

Meta Lead Ads, Page comments, and Instagram post interactions each generate a different kind of buyer signal — but without a bridge into your CRM, most teams lose them in the Meta Business Suite inbox. This module connects your Facebook Page and Instagram Business account through the crm.lead model, so every capture becomes a working opportunity your sales team already knows how to handle.

A dedicated platform configuration screen manages your Page tokens and webhook subscriptions, while post and comment views let you review social engagement without leaving Odoo. Convert any comment or lead directly into an opportunity with the built-in conversion wizard.

Process

From a Meta interaction to a working opportunity

1
Connect your Page & Instagram account

Configure your Facebook Page and Instagram Business account tokens once from Odoo Settings. The platform view keeps both connections in one place.

2
Leads and comments sync in

Lead Ads submissions, Page comments, and post interactions are captured automatically and stored against the originating platform record.

3
Convert to a CRM opportunity

Use the opportunity conversion wizard on any lead or comment to create a fully-formed CRM opportunity in one click, ready for your sales pipeline.

Features

Everything the module gives you
