Overview

A full WhatsApp desk, built on Tata Tele's Cloud API

This module connects Odoo CRM directly to the Tata Tele Business WhatsApp Cloud API, letting your team send and receive messages, auto-create or convert CRM opportunities straight from a conversation, and track session SLAs so no chat sits unanswered past your response window.

A second, fully independent WhatsApp line runs in parallel with the primary one - useful for separating sales and support numbers, or running two business lines without needing two Odoo instances.

Process

From an inbound WhatsApp message to a tracked deal

1
Connect your Tata WhatsApp lines

Configure your primary and, optionally, second WhatsApp Business number under Odoo Settings, each with its own session tracking.

2
Messages sync with SLA tracking

Inbound and outbound messages sync automatically, with session timers tracked so response SLAs are visible at a glance.

3
Opportunities are created or converted

Conversations auto-create CRM opportunities, or convert manually through the wizard - either way, nothing needs retyping.

Features

Everything the module gives you

Tata Tele Cloud API Connector

Send and receive WhatsApp Business messages through Tata Tele's official Cloud API, directly inside Odoo.

Dual WhatsApp Line Support

Run a second, independent WhatsApp line in parallel with your primary number - two desks, one Odoo.

Session SLA Tracking

Each WhatsApp session is timed, so your team can see at a glance which conversations are approaching a response deadline.

Auto Opportunity Creation

New WhatsApp conversations can automatically create a CRM opportunity, so no inbound message starts as untracked.

Opportunity Conversion Wizard

Manually convert any conversation into a CRM opportunity when auto-creation isn't the right fit.

Full Message History

Every WhatsApp exchange is stored against its CRM lead, giving full context to whoever picks up the conversation next.
