Overview

Your Google Business presence, mirrored inside Odoo

Google Reviews shape buying decisions long before a customer reaches your sales team, but that data usually lives outside your CRM entirely. This module connects Odoo to the Google Places API and pulls place details and review data directly into Odoo, using a dedicated sequence and review model so every rating, author, and comment is queryable alongside your CRM records.

A settings screen under Sales/CRM configuration manages your API key and place ID, and a menu entry gives your team a dedicated view of synced reviews - filterable and reportable the same way any other Odoo record is.

Process

From your Google listing to Odoo, in three steps

1
Add your API key & Place ID

Enter your Google Places API key and business Place ID once in Odoo's Settings under Sales/CRM configuration.

2
Reviews and place data sync

The module fetches place details and reviews from Google and stores them as sequenced records inside Odoo, ready to browse or report on.

3
Review from your CRM menu

Your team checks ratings, review text, and reviewer details from a dedicated Odoo menu - no separate login to Google Business required.
